## 📝 How to Use

### For Voters

1. **Login to the Portal**
   - Go to the [Login Page](http://localhost:3000/login)
   - ![Login Icon](public/MSU-TCTO.png)
   - Select your role and college, then enter your credentials.

2. **Access the Voter Dashboard**
   - View election status, reminders, and your voter information.
   - ![Dashboard](public/MSU-TCTO.png)

3. **Start Voting**
   - Click on the voting section for SSC or your College.
   - ![Voting Stepper](public/colleges/SSC.png)
   - Choose your candidates for each position. You can vote by party or individually.

4. **Review & Submit**
   - Review your selections before submitting.
   - Confirm your vote. You will see a success message.

5. **Get Your Digital Vote Receipt**
   - Download or print your vote receipt for verification.
   - ![Receipt Icon](public/candidates/no-photo.png)

6. **Logout**
   - Always logout after voting for security.

---

### For Admins

1. **Login as Admin**
   - Go to `/login` and select Admin role.

2. **Access Admin Dashboard**
   - View election statistics, turnout, and system status.
   - Manage candidates, parties, positions, and voters.

3. **Monitor Results**
   - View real-time results and statistics.
   - Export data and audit logs as needed.

---

## 📦 App Content Overview

- **Admin Panel:** `/admin/dashboard`  
  Manage the entire election process, including candidates, parties, positions, voters, and results.
- **Voter Dashboard:** `/voter/dashboard`  
  View election status, reminders, and cast your vote securely.
- **Voting Pages:** `/voter/vote/ssc` and `/voter/vote/college`  
  Step-by-step voting for SSC and College elections.
- **Results & Receipts:**  
  Real-time results and downloadable digital vote receipts for transparency and verification.
